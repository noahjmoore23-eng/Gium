Approach

How I work an account, start to close.

01

Preparation

I research an account before the first conversation — how it makes money, its cost structure, and the metrics its leadership reviews. Discovery starts from that, not from a script.

02

Qualification

I confirm need, budget, decision process, and timing on the first call, and say so on that call when one is missing rather than carrying the deal in a forecast.

03

Business case

My finance background lets me frame a proposal in the buyer’s own terms — cost per unit, payback period, and effect on next year’s budget — rather than as a feature comparison.

04

Close and handoff

I ran client operations for three years before I sold anything. I close on a written plan: a named decision-maker, dated steps, and a scope that can actually be delivered.
